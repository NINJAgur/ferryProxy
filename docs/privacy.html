Ferry — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Ferry has no user accounts. There is no sign-in, and we hold no name, email address or password.

What leaves your device

When you ask a question, the text of that question and the earlier messages in that conversation are sent to Ferry's relay server, which forwards them to the AI provider you selected:

  • Google (Gemini)
  • OpenAI (GPT)
  • Anthropic (Claude)

Their handling of that text is governed by their own policies, linked above.

What the relay keeps

  • Pieces of an answer, for about 5 minutes, so a piece lost on a weak connection can be fetched again. They are then discarded.
  • A device identifier generated at random by the app. It is not linked to you, your phone's hardware, or any account, and reinstalling the app produces a new one. It exists only to count free answers so the free tier is not exhausted by one device.
  • A purchase identifier, if you buy the add-on, along with how many paid answers you have used this month. This comes from the app store, not from us.

The relay keeps no record of your conversations.

What stays on your device

Your chats are written to a file on your own device and are not uploaded. Your settings and the bandwidth figures shown on the Data screen are also local. Deleting them in Settings, or uninstalling Ferry, removes them.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking software.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone beyond the AI provider needed to answer your question.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Children

Ferry is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete anything held about you: https://github.com/NINJAgur/ferryProxy/issues
